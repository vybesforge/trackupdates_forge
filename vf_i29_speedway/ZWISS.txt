Mid America Motorplex v0.9
==========================
I got to know this track in GTR2. 'Porsche Guy' had created this real track from the midlands for Sports Car GT and I really loved the flow. Unfortunately: although the layout was pretty accurate, the scenery was fantasy..
This version found its way to AC but since the scenery and athmosphare are totally wrong, I decided to remake the track in 3DS MAX from scratch.
I did use the outline of the main layout as a starting point and build everything up from scratch.
I hope you like it. I love the layout with Mazda MX, Porsche GT3 CUP etc..

Feel free to send me p.m. for suggestions or contributions.

The real track:
==============
The Mid America Motorplex is a relatively new club circuit located in Pacific Junction,
Iowa that was designed by world-renowned track designer Alan Wilson. 
The track is 2.23 miles long with 15 turns (increasing and decreasing radiuses),
long straights, many passing zones, a very challenging chicane near the pit entrance,
and a flat out kink." Lately it was renamed to 'Raceway Park of the Midlands'
In 2019 the whole track was flooded and afterwards a lot of work was done to recover from that.
FLOOD of 2019: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d--JCi5UiIU&list=WL&index=19
